# R.R.T ESCAPE: Lore and Missions
Callsign: **R.R.T Six** (Rapid Response Team). Radio voice: **OVERWATCH**. Shopkeeper: **QUARTERMASTER "DOC" HOLLOWAY**.
Setting: Site-Ember, a breached SCP Foundation facility. Zombies are called **"Hollows"** (staff changed by the anomaly **SCP-X-Lantern**, a gray mist).
All SCP numbers here are made up. Each line is one string, ready to paste into an array.

---

## MISSION 1: "LIGHTS OUT"
**Objectives**
1. Find a working keycard (Level 2) in Security Office B.
2. Restore backup power at the Generator Room.
3. Reach the Safe Zone in the East Cafeteria.

**Briefing**
> R.R.T Six, this is Overwatch. Your team's signals went dark when the containment doors failed. Site-Ember is on emergency power, and the halls are full of Hollows. Grab a keycard, get the generators running, and reach the cafeteria safe zone. Stay quiet. They follow sound.

**Radio lines**
- "Overwatch to Six. Security Office B is down the left corridor. Watch the corners."
- "Keycard acquired. Good. That opens the maintenance doors."
- "Hear that humming? That's the mist. Don't stay in it long."
- "Hollows don't see well in the dark. Don't let that make you brave."
- "Generator one is online. Two more to go."
- "Power's coming back. Every light that turns on, they'll notice."
- "Movement on my scanner. Lots of it. Move, Six. Move!"
- "Cafeteria doors are sealed but your card works. Get inside."
- "You made it. Catch your breath. Doc runs the supply terminal in there."

## MISSION 2: "THE WHISPERING WING"
**Objectives**
1. Get the Level 3 keycard from Dr. Ashby's office.
2. Recover 3 research drives from the Archive.
3. Seal the vent fan to stop the mist (hold the switch 30 seconds).
4. Return to the Safe Zone.

**Briefing**
> Six, the mist is coming from Research Wing C. The research drives in the Archive might tell us how to stop it. Dr. Ashby's office has the keycard you need. Fair warning: people say the wing whispers. Don't answer it.

**Radio lines**
- "You're in Wing C. Scanner's getting fuzzy. Stay close to the lights."
- "Ashby's office is on the second door to the right. The nameplate's still on it."
- "Did you hear your name just now? That wasn't me, Six."
- "Drive one recovered. Two left."
- "Those Hollows are wearing lab coats. They used to work here. Be careful."
- "Drive three. That's all of them. Now the vent fan."
- "Hold that switch! Hollows inbound. Thirty seconds!"
- "Fan sealed. Mist levels are dropping. Good work."
- "Head back to Doc. You've earned a resupply."

---

## MISSION 3: "SURFACE"
**Objectives**
1. Find the Level 4 keycard on the Site Director's desk.
2. Turn on the elevator in the Lower Lobby.
3. Survive the Hollows while the elevator comes down (90 seconds).
4. Reach the Helipad.

**Briefing**
> Six, this is it. The drives show the Lantern is pulling the Hollows toward the exits. The only way out is the main elevator to the helipad. Get the Director's keycard, call the elevator, and hold on until it arrives. Extraction is on its way. Let's get you home.

**Radio lines**
- "Director's office is behind the glass doors. Locked tight, but your Level 3 card should work."
- "Level 4 card. That's the master key, Six."
- "The elevator's calling down. Ninety seconds. Find a corner and hold it."
- "Sixty seconds. They're coming from every hallway."
- "Thirty! Don't stop now!"
- "Doors are open! Get in, get in!"
- "Going up. Look at all that mist below you. Don't look back."
- "Helipad's clear. I can see your light from the chopper."
- "Welcome back, Six. Mission complete. Site-Ember's quiet... for now."

## LORE DOCUMENTS (pickups)

**DOC-01: Containment Notice, SCP-X-Lantern**
> Object class: KETER. SCP-X-Lantern is an old brass lantern that gives off gray mist instead of light. Staff who stay in the mist too long start to wander and stop responding. Staff call them "Hollows." Do NOT light the lantern. Do NOT hum near it.

**DOC-02: Sticky Note on a Monitor**
> Whoever keeps humming in the hallway at night, PLEASE STOP. It's not funny. Nobody on the night shift hums. -M

**DOC-03: Dr. Ashby's Journal, Page 14**
> The lantern flickered today, even though it's sealed in glass. Then the mist leaked out through a crack I can't find. I think it wants to get out. I think it wants us to carry it.

**DOC-04: Cafeteria Menu (scribbled on)**
> Tuesday: Meatloaf. Wednesday: Tacos. Thursday: ~~Soup~~ CAFETERIA IS SAFE ZONE. DOORS LOCK FROM INSIDE. BRING FRIENDS.

**DOC-05: Security Log, 03:12 AM**
> Camera 7 showed Guard Pell walking in circles in Hall C for two hours. When we called him, he turned and looked straight into the camera. He didn't blink once. Sending an R.R.T unit.

**DOC-06: R.R.T Field Manual, Rule 9**
> If a Hollow says your name, keep moving. They don't actually know it. They're only repeating what they hear.

**DOC-07: Child's Drawing Taped to a Locker**
> A crayon drawing of a stick-figure family. Underneath it says: "Daddy works at the lantern place. He says he'll be home soon."

**DOC-08: Director's Memo (Classified)**
> If containment fails, seal the elevator and let the site go quiet. The Lantern cannot reach the surface. ...But if anyone is still alive down there, send R.R.T. Nobody gets left behind.
